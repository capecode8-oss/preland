Quick question. Who do you think lives longer, men or women? Most people guess right. But almost nobody can say why.

Here's what the numbers show. In the US in 2024, women lived to about 81.4 years. Men lived to about 76.5. That's a gap of nearly 5 years. Back in 1900, the gap was only about 2 years. Around 1980, it reached almost 8. Then it shrank again.

So the gap isn't fixed. It moves with time, and that's the clue. Part of the answer is simple: more men die before age 65, and that drags the average down. The rest is still debated. Habits, risks and biology all have supporters, and nobody has the full answer yet.

What we do know is that your body starts sending signals long before a real problem shows up. Most of us were never taught how to read them. That's the part that actually changes things.
