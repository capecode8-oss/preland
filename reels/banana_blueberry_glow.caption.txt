Quick one before you bake this. Everybody asks the same thing: is this actually healthy, or does it just sound healthy?

Here's the honest version. In the version shown, there's no flour. The bananas bring fiber and potassium. The yogurt adds protein. The blueberries bring antioxidants. That part is real.

But honey still counts as added sugar. So a cake is still a cake. Keep the slice small and treat it like dessert, not breakfast.

To make it work for you: use ripe bananas, and check the label on your yogurt. Sugar hides in the flavored kinds.

Two more things to check before you bake. First, taste the batter. Ripe bananas are sweeter, so you may need less honey. Second, look at how thick the batter is. If it looks more like soup than batter, add a little more yogurt and mix again.

Send this to the friend who always says she can't bake. It takes one bowl and about the same time as a sandwich.

When it comes out of the oven, let it cool before you slice it. Warm bread falls apart, and cool slices hold their shape. Then cut it into thin pieces so each one is a treat, not a meal.

Try it once as written. Then change one thing next time: more blueberries, less honey, or a different yogurt. Write down what you changed and how it tasted. That's how a recipe becomes yours.
