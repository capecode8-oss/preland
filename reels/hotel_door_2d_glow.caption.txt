Be honest. If you heard that knock, would you have opened the door?

Most of us would. Not because we're careless. Because we're kind. A voice at the door says she's locked out, and everything in us wants to help. That's the part worth knowing about.

Here's the simple version of what to do. Don't open the door, not even a crack. A chain on the door doesn't protect you once you open it. Look through the peephole. Then call the front desk yourself and tell them someone is knocking. They can send someone to the door. That's their job, and you never have to open it.

Some travelers also ask the front desk to write down their room number instead of saying it out loud in the lobby. It's a small thing, and it costs nothing.

None of this means every knock is a threat. Most of the time it really is a guest who made a mistake. The point is that you don't have to guess. You can stay kind and still stay safe, because you're helping through the door, not opening it.
