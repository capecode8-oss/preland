Think about the last time you stood at baggage claim. Where were you looking?

Most of us look everywhere except the belt. At the phone. At the arrivals board. For the people picking us up. We're tired, we just landed, and the belt feels like the safe part of the trip. It's actually the part where we're most distracted.

Police at several US airports have warned that bags get taken at baggage claim. It only takes someone grabbing the wrong bag and walking off. The good news is that a few small habits make it much harder.

Here's what I do now.

1. Go straight to the belt. Don't stop for coffee or the restroom first.
2. Stand where you can see your bag come out, and keep your phone in your pocket until you have it.
3. Put your valuables in your carry-on, not your checked bag.
4. Put your name and phone number on the bag, and add a bright tag so you can spot it from across the hall. A tracker inside helps too.
5. If your bag looks opened or feels lighter, don't walk out. Report it to the airline before you leave the airport. Some airlines want to hear within hours, so don't wait until you get home.
6. Keep your claim ticket until you're sure everything is there.

None of this is complicated. It's just paying attention for the few minutes when most of us don't.
