The number she'd never seen was printed on her statement every month. She just never read that box.

My friend Beth was paying $100 a month toward a $5,000 balance. She felt proud of it. She was also quietly stressed about the same card.

We sat down with her statement and a calculator.

1. The real math
$5,000 at 22% APR, paying $100 a month: about 137 payments. That's over 11 years and roughly $13,700 total. Most of it is interest.

2. The box on your statement
Under the CARD Act, your statement must show how many months it takes to pay the balance off at the minimum payment, and what monthly payment clears it in 3 years.

3. The 3-year number
For her balance, about $191 a month. Beth cut one subscription and one dinner out and found the money in a week.

She paid it off in three years.

When she told her brother, he said, "At your age, who cares about a credit card?" Beth said, "At my age, I'd like the last decade of my life to be mine."

Money is a plan. The same goes for your health. Beth said the scariest part of her statement wasn't the number. It was how long she'd looked at that page without reading it.

She taped the page to her fridge with the two numbers circled. Her husband said it was the first time he'd seen the whole picture. Sometimes the answer is already in the mail, in a box nobody reads.
