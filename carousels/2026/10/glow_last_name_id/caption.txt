Nobody in the family ever asked her why. She says she would have told them if they had.

My aunt Carol kept her ex-husband's last name for fourteen years after the divorce. At every holiday someone had a theory. Her sister said stubbornness. Her mother said pride. Carol just smiled and passed the potatoes.

Then her daughter turned 17 and went to get her first ID.

1. The clerk's question
"Same last name as your mom?" the clerk asked, looking at the old paperwork. The girl said yes without thinking.

2. The real reason
Carol had kept it so her daughter would never have to explain a different name at school, at the pediatrician's or at a friend's house. She told me she'd rather be misunderstood than make her kid answer questions.

3. What her daughter said
"Same as my mom's. She kept it for me." She'd known the whole time, and nobody had ever said it out loud.

I asked Carol what she wanted for herself, and she laughed. Then she didn't. She said, "That's the first time anyone's asked me that in fourteen years."
