Here's the routine I use now. It takes about ninety seconds.

Before you drive off, film the whole car. Slowly. Every side, the roof, the wheels, the inside. Say the date out loud.

Film the fuel gauge and the mileage. Disputes on those are common too.

Check your credit card before you fly. Many cards cover collision damage, but usually only if you decline the desk's insurance. Read the terms at home, not at the counter.

When you return the car, ask an employee to walk around it with you and sign off that there's no new damage. If the lot is empty, film everything again with the date.
