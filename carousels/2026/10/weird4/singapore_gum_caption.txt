Here's the part most people get wrong. Chewing gum is not illegal in Singapore. Selling it and bringing it in is.

The sales ban started in 1992. Regular gum, bubble gum, anything made from a gum base.

In 2004 a small exception was made after trade talks with the United States. Only gum with a medical or dental purpose can be sold, and only through a doctor or a registered pharmacist.

In 2010 the government said it stands by the ban. One customs-rules site says bringing in regular gum can mean a fine of up to 100,000 Singapore dollars. That's a third-party summary, so check the official customs page before you fly.

So the next time someone tells you "you can't chew gum there," you'll know the real rule is a lot stranger than that.
