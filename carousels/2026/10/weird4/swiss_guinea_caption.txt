It's a real rule. Since 2008, Switzerland has required guinea pigs to have company. Not as advice. As law.

The Swiss animal protection rules say guinea pigs are very social animals and may not be kept on their own. The country's food safety and veterinary office says exactly that.

It isn't only guinea pigs. Swiss sources list other animals that must live with at least one companion too, like some rats, mice and gerbils. The lists differ, so check the current rules if you ever own one.

The idea behind it is simple. Food, water and space are not enough for an animal that lives in groups. It needs company too.
