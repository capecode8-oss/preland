I must have looked shocked, because she explained. She said it isn't vanity. It's loyalty.

What she told me:

1. She's the one constant
"I'm the one person who has been there every single day of my life. Why would I treat her like an enemy?"

2. She never talks to herself like she'd never talk to a friend
When she catches a harsh thought, she asks: would I say this to someone I love?

3. She starts the day with one kind sentence
Before she gets out of bed, she says one kind thing about her body. Out loud. Even on bad days. Especially on bad days.
