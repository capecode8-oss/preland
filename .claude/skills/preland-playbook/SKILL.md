# Preland playbook (GLOW)

Sources used as reference (read, not installed): coreyhaines31/marketingskills (copywriting, cro, offers), plus Schwartz awareness levels, Hormozi value equation, Hopkins reason-why. Not used: robpalmer99/claude-code-copywriting-skills landing-page-copy, because its templates rely on invented authorities, invented user counts and "may be taken down" urgency.

## Structure (top to bottom, nothing important hidden behind tabs)
1. Hero: discomfort -> better future -> next step. One button.
2. "Sound like you?" five recognition lines.
3. What you walk away with (answers, next step, better doctor visit) + cost of waiting.
4. Why these guides (one question each, plain English, made to use).
5. Offer: quiz -> featured bundle -> other bundles -> single guides. Prices and buttons always visible.
6. Bonus tools, FAQ, guarantee, disclaimer, footer links.

## Copy rules
- Plain American English, 8th grade, short sentences, every medical word explained.
- Clear beats clever. Benefit before feature. One idea per section.
- No AI tells: no "It's not X, it's Y", no stacks of "No A. No B. No C.", no "Say goodbye to", no em dashes in headlines, no exclamation marks.
- Swap test: if a line would work on any competitor page, make it specific.
- CTA = verb + what you get ("GET THE CORE BUNDLE").

## Honesty rules (hard)
- No fake timers, no fake "regular price", no fake viewer or reader counts, no invented testimonials, no invented experts or studies.
- Strike-through or "bought one by one" prices are allowed ONLY if each item really sells at that price.
- Scarcity only if real: use CONFIG.launchNote with a real date and raise the price on that date.
- Health: educational wording only. No cure, no treat, no guaranteed results, no "doctors don't want you to know".
- Items that do not exist yet are marked "soon" and cannot be bought.

## Offer anatomy checklist
Core deliverable, bonus stack (the 4 tools), guarantee (14 days), real scarcity (optional), name, price. Test with 14 days of data: purchases per 100 clicks from Instagram.
