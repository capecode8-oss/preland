# QA checklist (run before showing the owner)
- Topic clear in 2 s, something moves at 0 s
- New visual event every 2-3 s, every claim has a visual
- Hero big (at least a third of the frame height), face readable on a phone
- No text or action in top 270 px or bottom 400 px except subtitles
- Subtitles match the audio words, numbers match the on-screen script
- Small honesty chips where needed ("Amounts vary by brand", "small study"), example values marked "example"
- No health-result claims, prices, "free", hashtags, music
- Loop: last 0.5 s matches the first shot (optional)
- Frames checked at 2 s, 12 s, 25 s, 40 s and the end; audio sync checked on 3 trigger words
