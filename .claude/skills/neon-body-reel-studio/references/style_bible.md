# Style bible: neon body silhouette (calm, premium, curious)

## Palette
bg gradient #0B1020 -> #16203F | rose (women) #FF8FB1 | cyan (men) #7DD3FC | teal #5EE6D3 | violet #A78BFA | amber (numbers, active word) #FFD27A | mint (good) #8BF0C1 | coral warning #FF9E7A | white text #FFFFFF | dim text #7C86A8
Never use pure or saturated red. If something is "dangerous", use coral + slow pulse, not red flashes.

## Background
Dark navy vertical gradient, 10-14 soft bokeh circles (violet/teal/rose, opacity 0.05-0.08, blur 22 px) drifting slowly. Nothing sharp in the background.

## Silhouettes
Two-pass outline (all glowing strokes first, all dark fills on top) so the figure is one clean neon outline: women rose, men cyan. Aura ellipse behind each figure, breathing with a slow sine (period 2.5 s). Figures stay in y 520-1180 so subtitles have room below.
Organs: glowing soft circles/blobs inside the body (heart, lungs, brain), pulse with the topic (heartbeat 1.1 s). Zoom-ins are done by scaling the group 1 -> 2.2 with ease.inOut over 0.8 s.

## Data visuals
Vertical rulers with amber ticks and one glowing marker (years, hours, counts). Numbers count up with ease.out over 1.2 s. Compare two things side by side (women vs men, before vs after). One big number per shot, 140+ px.

## Typography
Montserrat Bold. Title: 96 px, white with one amber keyword, y 300-450, visible first 3 s. Karaoke subtitles: 76 px, spoken words white, current word amber, upcoming words dim #7C86A8, 3 words per line, 2 lines, baseline y 1330, centered. Never both title and subtitles with the same words at once.

## Motion language
Draw-on and fade-in 0.25-0.4 s, ease.out; objects arrive with a tiny overshoot; hard cut on a new idea; slow push-in 4-6% on the key word; glow intensity rises on the key word and settles. A new visual event every 2-3 s.

## Shot patterns (pick per beat)
1 Question: title + two silhouettes + slow aura pulse
2 Compare: rulers rise to different heights, numbers count up
3 Zoom into an organ: figure scales up, organ glows, label chip appears
4 Timeline: horizontal bar with 3-5 dots, active dot glows amber (use real years/ages from the script only)
5 Myth vs fact: two chips, the myth fades to gray, the fact glows mint
6 Counter: one huge amber number counting
7 Closing: silhouettes dim, one rose-colored glowing word GLOW pill with a down arrow (CTA)

## Sound
Soft whoosh on cuts, glass "ting" on number reveals, low "thump" on a heartbeat beat, riser into the key reveal; 0.3 s silence before the reveal. SFX 10-14 dB under the voice. No music.
