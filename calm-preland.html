Why it keeps happening

Your body runs on a 4-hour cortisol cycle. At around 3AM, cortisol spikes — it's your built-in biological alarm designed to prepare you for the day. In most people it stays low. In people under stress, it fires too early — and jolts you awake.

Here's the part nobody tells you: the moment you start trying to fall back asleep, your brain reads that effort as a threat — and releases more cortisol. The harder you try, the more awake you become.

And every night you repeat this pattern, your brain learns it. It starts waking you at 3AM even on nights when the original stress is gone. That's why it's been months.
