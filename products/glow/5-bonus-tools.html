How to use the pack

  1. Read one guide. Do not read all four in a weekend.
  2. Pick two changes from it. Do them for two weeks.
  3. Fill in the 14-Day Body Log (Tool 2) every day. It takes 30 seconds.
  4. If you are still not right after 2 to 4 weeks, fill in the Doctor’s Brief (Tool 3) and take the 3 Questions (Tool 4) to your visit.

Do not wait if…

You have chest pain, trouble breathing, fainting, sudden severe belly pain, or a very fast or irregular heartbeat that does not settle. Get urgent care. These tools are not for emergencies, and nothing here is medical advice.

Tool 4 of 4

3 Questions For Your Doctor

Write down the answers. If you do not understand, say: “Can you say that in plain words?” That is always allowed.

1. What could be causing this, and what else should we rule out?

Why it matters: it makes your doctor name more than one possibility instead of “it is just stress” or “it is your age.”

2. Which tests make sense for me, and what will the results tell us?

You can ask about things like thyroid (TSH), iron stores (ferritin), blood sugar (A1C), or a sleep study. Ask which, if any, apply to you, and what a “normal” result means.

3. What should I try first, what should I track, and when should I come back?

Ask which warning signs mean you should call sooner, and when your next visit should be.
